# Client Intake Form

## Trade Log

- What platform created the file?
- Is it a live trade log or a backtest export?
- What is the initial capital?
- Are fees included?
- Is slippage included?
- Which column is trade ID?
- Which columns are entry and exit time?
- Which column shows long/short direction?

## Report

- Who will read the report?
- Do you need a PDF only, CSV outputs, or both?
- Should the report group by symbol, strategy, or market?
- Do you want one strategy or multiple strategies compared?

## Boundaries

- I do not need account credentials.
- I do not place trades.
- I do not guarantee profit.
- I only analyze the file provided for this project.
